FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I get in?

With your fingerprint. It's enrolled on the machine at the door when you join, and that's all you need to walk in.

How do I pay my fee?

Pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card and upload the receipt or transaction screenshot in the member app, or pay at the front desk. Once the gym approves it, your fee shows as paid.

What happens if my fee is due?

Your app shows your due date, and the fingerprint machine reminds you out loud when you punch in, so it never catches you by surprise.

Who makes my training and diet plan?

Your assigned coach. They build your training schedule and diet plan, and update them as you progress. You'll get a notification in the app when they do.

How do I get the member app?

Open app.olympicgym.pk on your phone and sign in with the details the front desk gives you. Use "Add to Home Screen" in your browser and it opens like a normal app.

Can I see my attendance and progress?

Yes. Your attendance history and your weight and BMI progress are all in the member app.
