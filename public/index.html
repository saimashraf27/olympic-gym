    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    I'm a beginner. Is Olympic Gym for me?

    Yes. Your coach starts from where you are today and builds your training and diet plan around your level and your goal.

    How do I join?

    Visit the front desk, choose your package and register. We enroll your fingerprint for entry and introduce you to your coach.

    Do I get a diet plan?

    Yes. Your coach sets a diet plan around your goal, whether that's losing weight, building muscle or general fitness, and updates it as you progress.

    How do I pay my fee?

    Pay at the front desk, or by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card and upload the receipt in the member app. Once it's approved your fee shows as paid.

    How do I get in?

    With your fingerprint. It's enrolled when you join, so there's no card to carry or forget.

    Can I track my progress?

    Yes. Your weight, BMI and attendance are recorded, and you can see them in the member app at app.olympicgym.pk.
